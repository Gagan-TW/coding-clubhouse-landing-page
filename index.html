Who We Are

At Coding Clubhouse, we believe every child deserves a chance to code. We are a community-powered initiative helping children build confidence through creative problem solving and computer science.

We give children that chance through outreach events, teacher training, and inclusive learning spaces, so kids see themselves as builders of technology—not just users of it.

What We Do

We design inclusive, beginner-friendly programs that introduce children and communities to the world of tech — no prior experience needed.

Featured Programs

Dream Beyond Limits

A hands-on outreach program that selects three promising students and one teacher from each participating school. We inspire them through coding sessions and leave behind our Code Equity curriculum so the whole school can keep learning.

function dreamBig() {
  const dreams = ['coding', 'creating', 'inspiring'];

    console.log('I can ' + dream + '!');
  });
}
