Who We Are

We are a volunteer-driven coding community that helps kids discover their voice through technology, projects, and teamwork.

Coding Clubhouse began as a small weekend club for curious kids who wanted to do more than just “use” technology. Today, it is a structured learning space where 8–16 year olds experiment with code, design interfaces, build games, and present their ideas.

Sessions are led by mentors from industry and academia who break concepts down into simple language, hands-on challenges, and collaborative projects. Each cohort ends with a showcase where kids demo what they’ve built to parents, teachers, and peers.

Our goal is not just to teach syntax, but to build confidence, problem-solving skills, and a growth mindset that stays with them beyond the classroom.
